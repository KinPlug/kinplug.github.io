Architecture

Integration services, and native plugins.

Most of the "Kintone plugin" market is actually external SaaS, requiring a second portal, a second login, and a second user directory. kinplug is different.

Integration-service model setup
  1. Create account on vendor portal (e.g. external portal)
  2. Leave Kintone, log into external portal
  3. Configure the rules in the external portal
  4. Return to Kintone, wire API keys in integration settings
  5. Every change means returning to the external portal
Admin UIs: 2 Logins: 2 Permissions: split
kinplug setup
  1. Open Kintone's plugin settings
  2. Configure
  3. Save
Admin UIs: 1 (Kintone) Logins: 1 Permissions: unified with Kintone

Everything is configured in Kintone's native plugin UI. No parallel permission tree to sync. No external API key management. Your IT admin's scope of responsibility doesn't grow.

FAQ

What buyers actually ask.

For anything not here, write support@kinplug.com. Same-day response in English, Japanese, or Tagalog.

Q01Are settings really all inside Kintone?
Yes. Open the plugin's settings panel and every configuration is there. There's no external portal to log into, no API key to manage. For Kinplug Mail, you authorize with Google or Microsoft once via OAuth — everything after that happens inside Kintone. Connection data is encrypted, stored in your own Kintone app (App 1434), and scoped per subdomain.
Q02Does it support cybozu.com (Japan Kintone)?
Fully. Same price, JPY billing available. Every plugin is tested on both kintone.com and cybozu.com domains. One of our active deployments runs across 43 apps on a Japanese financial group's cybozu.com environment.
Q03Our Microsoft 365 mail plugin just broke. Can you migrate us?
Yes, free migration support to Kinplug Mail. We preserve your existing templates, sender configurations, and recipient logic while switching the transport to OAuth 2.0. The 30-day trial lets you verify in your actual environment before committing. For urgent cases write support@kinplug.com directly.
Q04Are you a Cybozu Official Partner?
No. Edamame Inc. has been a Cybozu-recognized Partner of the Year since our consulting work began in 2019 — but we chose to keep the plugin business kinplug independent of the Official Partner channel. Independence means no partner-channel markup, no vendor-influenced product decisions, and neutral comparison when clients ask us what to use.
Q05What happens to my plugins if you shut down?
Every plugin except Kinplug Mail runs entirely inside your Kintone. They don't depend on our servers and will keep working forever. Kinplug Mail requires our OAuth infrastructure — that's the single exception. Our Standard and Enterprise contracts written commitment: if we ever cease operations, we open-source the API and help every customer migrate to self-hosted.
Q06Invoice billing, NET30, Japanese procurement docs?
Standard and Enterprise support Japanese procurement flow: PDF quote → PO received → invoice issued (NET30) → bank transfer. Annual contracts get 2 months free. We supply the documents your procurement team needs (company profile, security pack) for 稟議 approval. Japanese qualified invoice (適格請求書) registration is in progress for H1 2026.
Q07Who handles support?
The engineer who wrote the code. We chose to stay a small team deliberately — no tier-1 scripts, no ticket handoffs. Support runs from our Tokyo office, answered in English, Japanese, or Tagalog, same-day during JST business hours. Standard and Enterprise guarantee 4-hour response.
