Frequently asked questions

Will I be charged after the trial?

No. We don't even ask for your credit card. When the trial ends, the plugin simply stops working until you subscribe.

How many apps can I use it with?

Unlimited. One license covers your entire Kintone subdomain—all apps, all users, no restrictions.

Do I need programming skills?

Not at all. Everything is point-and-click. If you can use Kintone, you can use KinPlug.

Which Kintone plans work?

Standard plan or higher. Light plan doesn't support plugins (that's a Kintone limitation, not ours).

How fast is support?

We respond to emails within 24 hours, usually same-day. Annual plans get priority support with optional video calls.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

Yes. Annual plans come with a 30-day guarantee. Not happy? Full refund, no questions asked.
