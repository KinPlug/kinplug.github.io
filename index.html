Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need coding skills?

No! All plugins are designed for non-technical users with visual configuration interfaces.

What Kintone plans work?

Standard and Professional plans. Lite plan doesn't support plugins (Kintone limitation).

Is my data secure?

Yes. Plugins run in your Kintone. We never access or store your data.
