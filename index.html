Flow.

Visual automation with OAuth 2.0 email built in.

Google killed SMTP basic auth for Gmail in September 2024. Microsoft follows in April 2026. Most Kintone teams are paying Zapier just to send an email — or giving up entirely.

Flow replaces all of that. Gmail, Microsoft 365, shared mailboxes, CC/BCC, attachments from record fields, placeholder substitution, conditional triggers, AI actions. In Kintone. With a visual editor.

House rules

Three principles
01 · Use

We use what we sell.

Every plugin runs in production on our own consulting projects before it reaches the catalog. If it breaks for us, it doesn't ship to you.

02 · Ship

We ship when it's ready.

No demo-ware. No "coming soon" features hidden in pricing tiers. If it's listed as live, it works today, for every plan, in every language.

03 · Answer

We answer our own email.

Support goes to the people who wrote the code. No tier-1 script. English, Japanese, Tagalog — and usually within the same day.
