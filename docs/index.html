How to Install Plugins

  1. Download the plugin file - After starting a trial or purchasing, download the .zip file from your dashboard.
  2. Go to Kintone Administration - Click the gear icon → Kintone System Administration → Plugins.
  3. Upload the plugin - Click "Add Plugin" and upload the .zip file.
  4. Add to your app - Open your app settings → Plugins → Add the plugin.
  5. Configure - Open plugin settings and enter your license key.
  6. Deploy - Click "Update App" to activate the plugin.

Activating Your License

  1. Copy your license key from the dashboard or welcome email.
  2. Open your Kintone app → Settings → Plugins.
  3. Click the gear icon on the KinPlug plugin.
  4. Paste your license key in the "License Key" field.
  5. Click "Save" and deploy your app.

💡 One license works for all apps in your Kintone subdomain.

Starting a Free Trial

  1. Create an account at kinplug.com/login
  2. Go to your Dashboard
  3. Click "Start Trial" on the plugin you want to try.
  4. Enter your Kintone subdomain (e.g., "mycompany" from mycompany.kintone.com)
  5. Copy your trial license key and use it in Kintone.

💡 Trial is 14 days with full features. No credit card required.
