Privacy.

The short version. We collect only what we need to run the service: your email, your Kintone subdomain, your license keys, and telemetry about which plugin versions are installed. We don't sell data. We don't run ads. We don't share with marketing partners.

What we collect

Account data. Email address, name (if provided by SSO), and sign-in provider. Stored in Clerk.

License data. Your Kintone subdomain, which plugins you've trialed or purchased, license keys, activation timestamps. Stored in our Kintone license app (App 1370 on edamame.kintone.com).

OAuth tokens (Flow users only). Encrypted Gmail or Microsoft 365 access and refresh tokens. Stored in our OAuth app (App 1434) using AES-256 at rest. Scoped per-subdomain. Used only to send email on your behalf when triggered by your configured workflow.

Telemetry. Plugin version, Kintone subdomain, last-seen timestamp. Used to detect compatibility issues and notify of critical updates.

What we don't collect

We do not read your Kintone record content. Plugins run entirely in your browser against your Kintone — our servers never see record data, with one exception: Flow emails, which transit our API to reach Gmail or Microsoft's servers, and are not persisted on our side after the send completes.

Where data lives

Our API runs on Google Cloud Run in the asia-northeast1 region (Tokyo). License and OAuth data sits in Kintone (Japan, Tokyo region). Clerk authentication runs on their own infrastructure. For EU customers, Clerk supports EU data residency — ask us.

Your rights

Email support@kinplug.com to request a copy of your data, delete your account, or revoke OAuth connections. We respond within 30 days. Usually within 1.

Sub-processors

We use: Clerk (authentication), Google Cloud (hosting), Kintone / Cybozu (data storage), Tawk.to (live chat). That's it.

Contact

Questions: privacy@kinplug.com. We read every message.
