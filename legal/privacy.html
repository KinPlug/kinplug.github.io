Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 1, 2026

1. What we collect

kinplug collects: email address, name, company name, and Kintone subdomain at signup. OAuth tokens (encrypted) during plugin use. License validation logs and error logs. Payment information is handled by Stripe — we never store card details.

2. How we use it

Information is used only for: license validation, OAuth token refresh, service delivery, billing, troubleshooting, and responding to your inquiries.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your information to third parties. We share only with subprocessors necessary for service operation: Google Cloud Platform (infrastructure), Stripe (billing), Resend (transactional email), Cybozu (integration with your Kintone environment).

4. Storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored with industry-standard AES-256 encryption. Our servers are in the Tokyo region (asia-northeast1), and data stays in Japan by default. See Security for full details.

5. Your rights

You have the right to request disclosure, correction, or deletion of your personal information. Write to legal@kinplug.com. We respond within 14 days.

6. Cookies

kinplug.com uses cookies only for login session persistence, language preference memory, and basic service delivery. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

7. Changes

We notify registered email addresses at least 30 days before any significant change to this policy. Change history is published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or concerns: legal@kinplug.com. Operated by Edamame Inc., Tom Arai, representative.
