Refunds

Refund Policy

Last updated: April 21, 2026

1. Overview

kinplug (the "Service") is operated by Edamame Inc. (the "Company"). This Refund Policy explains when and how customers may receive a refund for paid subscriptions to the Service. This policy supplements and should be read together with our Terms of Service.

2. 30-day free trial

Every plan includes a 30-day free trial with full feature access. No payment method is charged during the trial. If the customer does not wish to continue after the trial, they may cancel at any time before the trial ends and will not be billed. Because no payment is taken during the trial, no refund is necessary.

3. Monthly subscriptions

Monthly subscriptions may be cancelled at any time from the customer's account dashboard. Upon cancellation, the subscription remains active through the end of the current billing month, and no further charges are made. Partial-month refunds are not issued for monthly subscriptions, because the customer retains full access to the Service for the period already paid.

4. Annual subscriptions — refund eligibility

Annual subscriptions are eligible for a full refund under the following conditions:

  1. Post-trial cancellation window. The customer requests cancellation within 7 days after the 30-day free trial ends (i.e., within 21 days of initial signup).
  2. Material breach by Edamame Inc. The Company materially fails to deliver the Service as described, and the failure is not remedied within a reasonable period after written notice.
  3. Extended service outage. The Service experiences an outage exceeding 30 consecutive days without recovery, and the customer elects not to continue.

Outside of these conditions, annual subscriptions are non-refundable mid-term. This reflects the discounted annual rate (annual plans include 2 free months compared to monthly billing).

5. How to request a refund

To request a refund, email billing@kinplug.com with:

  • The email address associated with the account
  • The order ID or invoice number
  • The reason for the refund request

We aim to respond to all refund requests within 2 business days (JST). Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5–10 business days, subject to the payment processor's standard timelines.

6. Non-refundable situations

Refunds are not available in the following cases:

  • Requests received outside the eligibility conditions in Section 4
  • Accounts suspended or terminated for violations of the Terms of Service (including reverse-engineering, resale, or interference with Service operations)
  • Monthly subscription periods that have already started
  • Usage-based charges or third-party costs incurred through the Service

7. Chargebacks

If a customer disputes a charge with their card issuer without first contacting us, we ask that they reach out to billing@kinplug.com so we can resolve the matter directly. Most issues are resolved more quickly outside the chargeback process. If a chargeback is filed in bad faith or in contradiction of this policy, the Company reserves the right to suspend the associated account pending resolution.

8. Company shutdown

In the event the Company ceases operation of the Service, the customer protections described in Section 6 of the Terms of Service apply (including 180 days' notice, open-sourcing of the kinplug Mail API server, and self-hosting migration assistance). Refunds of unused subscription periods will be processed on a pro-rata basis where technically feasible.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced at least 30 days before taking effect, and will not apply retroactively to subscriptions already purchased.

10. Contact

Refund requests and billing questions: billing@kinplug.com.
Legal questions about this policy: legal@kinplug.com.
