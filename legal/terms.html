Licensing

Each license grants use on one Kintone subdomain for the billed period, for unlimited users on that subdomain. Licenses are non-transferable between subdomains without written notice.

Trials

14-day trials require no payment method. At end of trial, the plugin displays a notice on the configuration screen and stops performing its active functions (sending emails, rendering PDFs, overriding lookup modals). Your Kintone data is never altered or deleted.

Monthly plans bill on the anniversary of signup. Annual plans available for Enterprise, billed once a year. Cancel any time — you're billed through the end of the current period; no prorated refunds. Refunds for service failure are granted at our discretion and have been granted every time asked.
