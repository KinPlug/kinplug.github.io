Features

Mail and automation, one plugin.

Auth
Full OAuth 2.0

Gmail (including Workspace) and Microsoft 365 (including Exchange Online) via OAuth 2.0. We never store your SMTP password. Auto token refresh. Neither Google's nor Microsoft's auth shutdowns touch you.

Sender
Send As & shared mailboxes

Proper Send As via Microsoft 365 Exchange Admin delegation. Send from personal mailboxes with delegation, or directly from shared mailboxes. Both modes supported.

Automation
Visual flow builder

Triggers (record added, status changed, scheduled), conditions, chained actions — designed in GUI. Zapier/Make-equivalent automation running inside your Kintone.

Attach
Per-field attachment selection

Choose exactly which attachment fields get included. "Attach the quote PDF but not the internal-notes PDF" — that specific. Dynamically-generated PDFs from PDF Pro are supported.

Bulk
10,000+ record bulk sends

List-view bulk send with query conditions. We bypass Kintone's 10,000-record API offset limit with a custom pagination routine, so large campaigns ship in one click. Logs persist in your Kintone.

Schedule
Timezone-aware scheduling

Point-in-time scheduling, recurring rules ("first of month", "Friday 5pm"), and duplicate-run locks. Designed timezone-aware from day one. Accurate in JST, UTC, or anything else.

Setup

Migrate from your existing plugin in 90 seconds.

Kinplug Mail installs from Kintone's plugin management UI by uploading a .zip — then you authorize OAuth with Google or Microsoft once. That's the setup. If you're migrating from another mail plugin, we preserve your templates, recipient logic, and sender configurations.

The 30-day trial runs in your actual environment with full feature access. If your procurement team needs specific documents for approval, tell us what you need via the contact form.

Compared with kMailer

kMailer, from Toyokumo, is the established mail product in the Kintone ecosystem and genuinely strong at campaign mail — HTML editing, open-rate analytics and A/B testing. If marketing email is your primary need, it is a serious option. The architectural difference matters most for business mail:

  • Where mail is sent from — Kinplug Mail authenticates with OAuth 2.0 and sends through your own Gmail or Microsoft 365 mailbox, including Send As and shared mailboxes. Replies land in that mailbox, and the message sits in your own Sent folder.
  • Pricing — Toyokumo publishes kMailer at ¥15,000/month Standard and ¥24,000/month Premium. Kinplug Mail is not priced separately: one subscription covers every Kinplug plugin.
  • Authentication deadline — Google ended Gmail basic auth in 2024 and Microsoft is retiring it for Exchange Online. Password-based Kintone mail customisations stop working; OAuth 2.0 is the supported path.

Competitor pricing above is as published by Toyokumo at the time of writing; check their site for current figures.
