Frequently Asked Questions

How does the free trial work?

Your 14-day trial starts when you install the plugin and enter your trial license key. You get full access to all features during the trial period. No credit card required.

Is the license per user or per app?

Licenses are per Kintone subdomain (environment). You can use the plugin in multiple apps within the same Kintone environment with a single license.

Can I switch between monthly and annual?

Yes! You can upgrade from monthly to annual at any time. When you upgrade, you'll receive credit for any unused time on your monthly plan.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express) and PayPal through our secure payment processor.

What happens when my subscription expires?

The plugin will continue to work in read-only mode. You'll be able to view existing PDFs but won't be able to generate new ones until you renew your subscription.
