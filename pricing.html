Questions, answered

Frequently · honestly

What happens when my trial ends?

The plugin stops working on day 15 and shows a message on the config screen. Your data stays intact. Activate a paid license and it resumes exactly where it left off. No dark patterns, no hidden billing.

Do I need one license per app?

No. Licenses are per-subdomain, per-plugin. Install Flow on as many apps as you like within mycompany.kintone.com — one license covers all of them.

Can we self-host?

Most plugins run entirely in the browser and require no external service. Flow, which needs OAuth 2.0 for Gmail and Microsoft 365, calls our API on Google Cloud Run (Tokyo). Enterprise customers can request an on-premise proxy deployment — ask us.

What's your refund policy?

You get 14 days before you pay. After that, cancel any time and you'll be billed through the end of the month — no charges after. If something breaks and we can't fix it, we'll refund. Just email us.
