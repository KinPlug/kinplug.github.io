Commitments

Written into the contract.

Price preserved for existing customers

Rate changes never apply retroactively. Current customers can renew at current rates for at least 24 months after any announced change.

No per-user or usage metering, ever

Flat-rate is architectural, not promotional. We will never add per-user, per-app, or API-count pricing to existing plans.

Open-source on shutdown

If kinplug ever ceases operations, the Kinplug Mail API server gets open-sourced, and we commit to helping every customer migrate to self-hosted — written in every Standard and Enterprise contract.

Direct, not via Cybozu partner channel

We sell direct, not through the Cybozu Official Partner channel. No intermediary markup means vendor-neutral product decisions and stable pricing.
