Pricing FAQ

Can I use one license for multiple apps?

Yes! One license covers all apps within your Kintone subdomain. Unlimited users too.

What payment methods do you accept?

Credit card, bank transfer, and invoice billing. Annual plans support invoice payment.

Will I be charged after the trial?

No. The trial automatically expires. You need to sign up separately to continue.

What's your refund policy?

Annual plans have a 30-day money-back guarantee. Monthly plans are non-refundable.

What if I need multiple plugins?

Each plugin requires its own license. Contact us for multi-plugin discounts.

Can I change my subdomain?

Yes. Trial allows 1 change, monthly 1/month, annual 2/year. Contact support for more.
